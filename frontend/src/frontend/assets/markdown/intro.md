Welcome to **eClipseBord** - a small dashboard about **solar eclipses**.

The data comes from NASA's five millennium catalog of solar eclipses, covering
thousands of years of eclipse events (from year -1999 onwards).

Use the pages in the left sidebar:

- **Charts** - graphs about eclipse types, time periods and locations
- **Data** - browse the raw dataset

The numbers below come live from the FastAPI backend.
